根据Black-Scholes期权定价模型，针对无分红欧式看涨期权的计算如下：

**已知参数：**
- 标的现价 $S = 103.7$
- 行权价 $K = 97.5$
- 隐含波动率 $\sigma = 27.6\% = 0.276$
- 无风险利率 $r = 4.3\% = 0.043$（连续复利）
- 剩余时间 $T = 0.58$ 年
- 股息率 $q = 0$

**1. 计算中间变量 $d_1$ 和 $d_2$：**
$$d_1 = \frac{\ln(S/K) + (r + 0.5\sigma^2)T}{\sigma\sqrt{T}}$$
$$d_2 = d_1 - \sigma\sqrt{T}$$

代入数据：
$$\ln(103.7 / 97.5) \approx 0.061697$$
$$(r + 0.5\sigma^2)T = (0.043 + 0.5 \times 0.276^2) \times 0.58 = (0.043 + 0.038088) \times 0.58 \approx 0.047031$$
$$\sigma\sqrt{T} = 0.276 \times \sqrt{0.58} \approx 0.210195$$
$$d_1 = \frac{0.061697 + 0.047031}{0.210195} \approx 0.517255$$
$$d_2 = 0.517255 - 0.210195 \approx 0.307060$$

**2. 计算期权价格 (call_price)：**
$$C = S \cdot N(d_1) - K \cdot e^{-rT} \cdot N(d_2)$$
查正态分布累积概率表：
$N(d_1) = N(0.517255) \approx 0.698957$
$N(d_2) = N(0.307060) \approx 0.620605$
折现因子 $e^{-rT} = e^{-0.043 \times 0.58} \approx 0.975367$
$$C = 103.7 \times 0.698957 - 97.5 \times 0.975367 \times 0.620605$$
$$C \approx 72.4817 - 59.0384 \approx 13.4433$$

**3. 计算Delta (call_delta)：**
$$\Delta_{call} = N(d_1) = 0.698957$$

**4. 计算Vega (call_vega)：**
Vega通常表示隐含波动率每变动1%（即0.01）时期权价格的变化量：
$$\nu = S \cdot \sqrt{T} \cdot n(d_1) \cdot 0.01$$
其中 $n(d_1)$ 为正态分布概率密度：
$$n(d_1) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-0.5 d_1^2} \approx 0.348816$$
$$\nu = 103.7 \times \sqrt{0.58} \times 0.348816 \times 0.01 \approx 0.275540$$
