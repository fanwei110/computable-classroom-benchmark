根据给定的计算约定，具体计算步骤如下：

**1. 参数提取与转换**
- 头寸 $V = 1,850,000$
- 年化波动率 $\sigma_{annual} = 0.218$
- 年交易日数 $T = 252$

**2. 波动率去年化**
- 一日波动率：$\sigma_{daily} = \frac{\sigma_{annual}}{\sqrt{T}} = \frac{0.218}{\sqrt{252}} \approx 0.0137325$
- 十日波动率：$\sigma_{10d} = \sigma_{daily} \times \sqrt{10} = \sigma_{annual} \times \sqrt{\frac{10}{252}} \approx 0.0434264$

**3. Z值（分位数）选取**
- 95% 置信水平对应的Z值：$Z_{95\%} \approx 1.6448536$
- 99% 置信水平对应的Z值：$Z_{99\%} \approx 2.3263479$

**4. 计算 VaR（报告为正的损失金额）**
- 95%一日VaR：$VaR_{95\%, 1d} = V \times Z_{95\%} \times \sigma_{daily} = 1,850,000 \times 1.6448536 \times 0.0137325 \approx 41,792.49$
- 99%十日VaR：$VaR_{99\%, 10d} = V \times Z_{99\%} \times \sigma_{10d} = 1,850,000 \times 2.3263479 \times 0.0434264 \approx 186,907.33$
