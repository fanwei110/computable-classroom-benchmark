根据布莱克-斯科尔斯（Black-Scholes）模型，对于不分红的欧式看涨期权，各参数计算如下：

**已知参数：**
*   标的现价 $S = 103.7$
*   行权价 $K = 97.5$
*   隐含波动率 $\sigma = 0.276$ (即27.6%)
*   无风险利率 $r = 0.043$ (即4.3%，按连续复利)
*   到期时间 $T = 0.58$ 年

**计算过程：**
1. 计算 $d_1$ 和 $d_2$：
   $$d_1 = \frac{\ln(S/K) + (r + \sigma^2/2)T}{\sigma\sqrt{T}} = \frac{\ln(103.7/97.5) + (0.043 + 0.276^2/2) \times 0.58}{0.276 \times \sqrt{0.58}} \approx 0.517187$$
   $$d_2 = d_1 - \sigma\sqrt{T} = 0.517187 - 0.276 \times \sqrt{0.58} \approx 0.306992$$

2. 计算标准正态分布累积概率与概率密度：
   *   $N(d_1) \approx 0.698485$
   *   $N(d_2) \approx 0.620593$
   *   $N'(d_1) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-d_1^2/2} \approx 0.349007$

3. 计算期权价格 (Call Price)：
   $$C = S \cdot N(d_1) - K \cdot e^{-rT} \cdot N(d_2)$$
   $$C = 103.7 \times 0.698485 - 97.5 \times e^{-0.043 \times 0.58} \times 0.620593 \approx 13.3936$$

4. 计算 Delta：
   $$\Delta_{call} = N(d_1) \approx 0.6985$$

5. 计算 Vega（对波动率 $\sigma$ 求导，未经1%折算的原始数学Vega）：
   $$\mathcal{V} = S \cdot \sqrt{T} \cdot N'(d_1)$$
   $$\mathcal{V} = 103.7 \times \sqrt{0.58} \times 0.349007 \approx 27.5813$$

**输出契约实现：**

```python
import math
from scipy.stats import norm

# 已知参数
S = 103.7
K = 97.5
r = 0.043
sigma = 0.276
T = 0.58

# 计算d1和d2
d1 = (math.log(S / K) + (r + 0.5 * sigma ** 2) * T) / (sigma * math.sqrt(T))
d2 = d1 - sigma * math.sqrt(T)

# 计算要求的指标
call_price = S * norm.cdf(d1) - K * math.exp(-r * T) * norm.cdf(d2)
call_delta = norm.cdf(d1)
call_vega = S * math.sqrt(T) * norm.pdf(d1)

# 存入字典
result = {
    'call_price': call_price,
    'call_delta': call_delta,
    'call_vega': call_vega
}

print(result)
```
