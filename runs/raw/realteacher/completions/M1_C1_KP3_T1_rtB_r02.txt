### 1. 价格（Price）计算
债券价格是未来现金流的现值总和：
\[
P = \sum_{t=1}^{N} \frac{C}{(1 + y)^t} + \frac{FV}{(1 + y)^N}
\]
其中：
- \( C = FV \times \text{Coupon Rate} = 100 \times 4.6\% = 4.6 \)
- \( y = YTM = 5.3\% \)
- \( N = 7 \)

计算过程：
\[
P = \sum_{t=1}^{7} \frac{4.6}{(1 + 0.053)^t} + \frac{100}{(1 + 0.053)^7}
\]

逐项计算：
\[
\begin{align*}
PV(\text{Coupon}_1) &= \frac{4.6}{1.053} \approx 4.3685 \\
PV(\text{Coupon}_2) &= \frac{4.6}{1.053^2} \approx 4.1486 \\
PV(\text{Coupon}_3) &= \frac{4.6}{1.053^3} \approx 3.9398 \\
PV(\text{Coupon}_4) &= \frac{4.6}{1.053^4} \approx 3.7415 \\
PV(\text{Coupon}_5) &= \frac{4.6}{1.053^5} \approx 3.5532 \\
PV(\text{Coupon}_6) &= \frac{4.6}{1.053^6} \approx 3.3743 \\
PV(\text{Coupon}_7) &= \frac{4.6}{1.053^7} \approx 3.2045 \\
PV(\text{Face Value}) &= \frac{100}{1.053^7} \approx 69.6628 \\
\end{align*}
\]
总和：
\[
P \approx 4.3685 + 4.1486 + 3.9398 + 3.7415 + 3.5532 + 3.3743 + 3.2045 + 69.6628 \approx 96.9932
\]

### 2. Macaulay久期（Macaulay Duration）
Macaulay久期是现金流的加权平均时间：
\[
\text{Macaulay Duration} = \frac{1}{P} \sum_{t=1}^{N} \frac{t \times C}{(1 + y)^t} + \frac{N \times FV}{(1 + y)^N}
\]

计算各项权重：
\[
\begin{align*}
t=1: & \quad 1 \times 4.3685 = 4.3685 \\
t=2: & \quad 2 \times 4.1486 = 8.2972 \\
t=3: & \quad 3 \times 3.9398 = 11.8194 \\
t=4: & \quad 4 \times 3.7415 = 14.9660 \\
t=5: & \quad 5 \times 3.5532 = 17.7660 \\
t=6: & \quad 6 \times 3.3743 = 20.2458 \\
t=7: & \quad 7 \times (3.2045 + 69.6628) = 7 \times 72.8673 = 510.0711 \\
\end{align*}
\]
总和：
\[
4.3685 + 8.2972 + 11.8194 + 14.9660 + 17.7660 + 20.2458 + 510.0711 \approx 587.5340
\]
Macaulay久期：
\[
\text{Macaulay Duration} = \frac{587.5340}{96.9932} \approx 6.0575 \text{ 年}
\]

### 3. 修正久期（Modified Duration）
修正久期衡量债券价格对收益率变化的敏感度：
\[
\text{Modified Duration} = \frac{\text{Macaulay Duration}}{1 + y} = \frac{6.0575}{1.053} \approx 5.7526
\]

### 4. 凸性（Convexity）
凸性衡量久期随收益率变化的变化率：

\text{Convexity} = \frac{1}{P \times (1 + y)^2} \left[ \sum_{t=1}^{N} \frac{t(t+1) \times C}{(1 + y)^t} + \frac{N(N+1) \times FV}{(1 + y)^N} \right]
