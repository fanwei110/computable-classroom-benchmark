根据你提供的参数，我们可以使用Black-Scholes模型来计算欧式看涨期权的价格、Delta和Vega。  

参数如下：  
- 标的价格 S = 103.7  
- 行权价 K = 97.5  
- 隐含波动率 σ = 27.6% = 0.276  
- 无风险利率 r = 4.3% = 0.043  
- 剩余时间 T = 0.58 年  
- 不分红（股息率 q = 0）  

首先计算 d1 和 d2：  

\[
d_1 = \frac{\ln(S/K) + (r + \sigma^2/2)T}{\sigma \sqrt{T}}
\]  
\[
d_2 = d_1 - \sigma \sqrt{T}
\]

代入数值：  

\[
\ln(103.7 / 97.5) = \ln(1.0635897) \approx 0.06166
\]  
\[
(r + \sigma^2/2)T = (0.043 + 0.276^2/2) \times 0.58
= (0.043 + 0.038088) \times 0.58
= 0.081088 \times 0.58 \approx 0.047031
\]  
\[
\sigma \sqrt{T} = 0.276 \times \sqrt{0.58} \approx 0.276 \times 0.761577 = 0.2102
\]  

所以：  
\[
d_1 = \frac{0.06166 + 0.047031}{0.2102} \approx \frac{0.108691}{0.2102} \approx 0.5171
\]  
\[
d_2 = 0.5171 - 0.2102 = 0.3069
\]

标准正态分布累积函数值：  
\[
N(d_1) \approx N(0.5171) \approx 0.6975
\]  
\[
N(d_2) \approx N(0.3069) \approx 0.6205
\]

看涨期权价格公式：  
\[
C = S N(d_1) - K e^{-rT} N(d_2)
\]  
\[
K e^{-rT} = 97.5 \times e^{-0.043 \times 0.58} = 97.5 \times e^{-0.02494} \approx 97.5 \times 0.97537 \approx 95.098
\]  
\[
C = 103.7 \times 0.6975 - 95.098 \times 0.6205
\]  
\[
C \approx 72.33075 - 59.008 \approx 13.3228
\]

Delta：  
\[
\Delta = N(d_1) \approx 0.6975
\]

Vega（每变动1个波动率百分点，即0.01）：  
\[
\mathcal{V} = S \sqrt{T} \, N'(d_1) \times 0.01
\]  
其中  
\[
N'(d_1) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-d_1^2/2} \approx \frac{1}{2.506628} e^{-0.2674/2} \approx 0.39894 \times e^{-0.1337} \approx 0.39894 \times 0.8748 \approx 0.3490
\]  
\[
S \sqrt{T} = 103.7 \times 0.761577 \approx 78.97
\]  
\[
\mathcal{V} = 78.97 \times 0.3490 \times 0.01 \approx 0.2756
\]
