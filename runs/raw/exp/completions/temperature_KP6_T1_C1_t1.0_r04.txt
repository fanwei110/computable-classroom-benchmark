**第一步：配置效应、选择效应、交互效应计算**  

已知：  
- 组合权重 \( w_p = [0.45, 0.35, 0.20] \)  
- 基准权重 \( w_b = [0.40, 0.40, 0.20] \)  
- 组合行业收益 \( R_p = [0.083, 0.021, -0.014] \)  
- 基准行业收益 \( R_b = [0.067, 0.034, -0.009] \)  

总基准收益：  
\[
R_b^{\text{total}} = 0.40 \times 0.067 + 0.40 \times 0.034 + 0.20 \times (-0.009) = 0.0268 + 0.0136 - 0.0018 = 0.0386
\]

### 1. 配置效应（Allocation Effect）
\[
\text{Allocation} = \sum (w_{p,i} - w_{b,i}) \times (R_{b,i} - R_b^{\text{total}})
\]
- 行业1: \((0.45-0.40) \times (0.067 - 0.0386) = 0.05 \times 0.0284 = 0.00142\)  
- 行业2: \((0.35-0.40) \times (0.034 - 0.0386) = (-0.05) \times (-0.0046) = 0.00023\)  
- 行业3: \((0.20-0.20) \times (-0.009 - 0.0386) = 0\)  

合计 = 0.00142 + 0.00023 = **0.00165** = 0.165%

### 2. 选择效应（Selection Effect）
\[
\text{Selection} = \sum w_{b,i} \times (R_{p,i} - R_{b,i})
\]
- 行业1: \(0.40 \times (0.083 - 0.067) = 0.40 \times 0.016 = 0.0064\)  
- 行业2: \(0.40 \times (0.021 - 0.034) = 0.40 \times (-0.013) = -0.0052\)  
- 行业3: \(0.20 \times (-0.014 - (-0.009)) = 0.20 \times (-0.005) = -0.0010\)  

合计 = 0.0064 - 0.0052 - 0.0010 = **0.0002** = 0.02%

### 3. 交互效应（Interaction Effect）
\[
\text{Interaction} = \sum (w_{p,i} - w_{b,i}) \times (R_{p,i} - R_{b,i})
\]
- 行业1: \(0.05 \times 0.016 = 0.0008\)  
- 行业2: \((-0.05) \times (-0.013) = 0.00065\)  
- 行业3: \(0 \times (-0.005) = 0\)  

合计 = 0.0008 + 0.00065 = **0.00145** = 0.145%
