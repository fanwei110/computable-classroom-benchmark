好的，我们来一步一步计算你给出的债券数据。  
已知条件：  

- 面值 \( F = 100 \)  
- 票面利率 \( c = 4.6\% \)（每年付息一次）  
- 期限 \( T = 7 \) 年  
- 到期收益率 \( y = 5.3\% \)（每年计息一次）  

---

**第一步：计算债券价格**  

每年利息 \( C = 100 \times 4.6\% = 4.6 \)  
价格公式为：

\[
P = \sum_{t=1}^{7} \frac{4.6}{(1+0.053)^t} + \frac{100}{(1+0.053)^7}
\]

我们先算每项：

各年利息现值：

- t=1: \( 4.6 / 1.053 = 4.36847 \)  
- t=2: \( 4.6 / 1.053^2 = 4.14859 \)  
- t=3: \( 4.6 / 1.053^3 = 3.93967 \)  
- t=4: \( 4.6 / 1.053^4 = 3.74127 \)  
- t=5: \( 4.6 / 1.053^5 = 3.55305 \)  
- t=6: \( 4.6 / 1.053^6 = 3.37432 \)  
- t=7: \( 4.6 / 1.053^7 = 3.20448 \)  

本金现值：  
\( 100 / 1.053^7 = 100 / 1.43428 = 69.7189 \)

加总：

利息现值总和 ≈ 4.36847 + 4.14859 + 3.93967 + 3.74127 + 3.55305 + 3.37432 + 3.20448 = 26.32985  

价格 \( P = 26.32985 + 69.7189 = 96.04875 \)

所以：
\[
\text{price} \approx 96.0488
\]

---

**第二步：Macaulay久期（年）**  

公式：
\[
D_{\text{mac}} = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{7} t \cdot \frac{CF_t}{(1+y)^t}}{P}
\]

各期权重分子：

- t=1: \( 1 \times 4.36847 = 4.36847 \)  
- t=2: \( 2 \times 4.14859 = 8.29718 \)  
- t=3: \( 3 \times 3.93967 = 11.81901 \)  
- t=4: \( 4 \times 3.74127 = 14.96508 \)  
- t=5: \( 5 \times 3.55305 = 17.76525 \)  
- t=6: \( 6 \times 3.37432 = 20.24592 \)  
- t=7: \( 7 \times (3.20448 + 69.7189) = 7 \times 72.92338 = 510.46366 \)  

总和 = 4.36847 + 8.29718 = 12.66565  
再加 11.81901 = 24.48466  
再加 14.96508 = 39.44974  
再加 17.76525 = 57.21499  
再加 20.24592 = 77.46091  
再加 510.46366 = 587.92457  

Macaulay久期：
\[
D_{\text{mac}} = \frac{587.92457}{96.04875} \approx 6.1211 \text{ 年}
\]

---

**第三步：修正久期**  

\[
D_{\text{mod}} = \frac{D_{\text{mac}}}{1+y} = \frac{6.1211}{1.053} \approx 5.8130
\]

**第四步：凸性**  

凸性公式（每年付息一次）：
\[
\text{Convexity} = \frac{1}{P(1+y)^2} \sum_{t=1}^{7} t(t+1) \frac{CF_t}{(1+y)^t}
\]

先计算各期 \( t(t+1) \times PV(CF_t) \)：

- t=1: \( 1\times2 \times 4.36847 = 8.73694 \)  
- t=2: \( 2\times3 \times 4.14859 = 24.89154 \)  
- t=3: \( 3\times4 \times 3.93967 = 47.27604 \)  
- t=4: \( 4\times5 \times 3.74127 = 74.8254 \)  
- t=5: \( 5\times6 \times 3.55305 = 106.5915 \)  
- t=6: \( 6\times7 \times 3.37432 = 141.72144 \)  
- t=7: \( 7\times8 \times (3.20448+69.7189) = 56 \times 72.92338 = 4083.70928 \)  

总和 = 8.73694 + 24.89154 = 33.62848  
+ 47.27604 = 80.90452  
+ 74.8254 = 155.72992  
+ 106.5915 = 262.32142  
+ 141.72144 = 404.04286  
+ 4083.70928 = 4487.75214  

凸性：
\[
\text{Convexity} = \frac{4487.75214}{96.04875 \times (1.053)^2}
\]
先算分母： \( 1.053^2 = 1.108809 \)  
\( 96.04875 \times 1.108809 = 106.498 \)  
所以：
\[
\text{Convexity} \approx \frac{4487.75214}{106.498} \approx 42.140
