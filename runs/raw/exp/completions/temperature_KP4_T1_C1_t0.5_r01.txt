根据你给出的参数，我们用Black-Scholes模型计算欧式看涨期权的价格、Delta和Vega。  

参数汇总：  
- 标的现价 S = 103.7  
- 行权价 K = 97.5  
- 隐含波动率 σ = 27.6% = 0.276  
- 无风险利率 r = 4.3% = 0.043  
- 剩余时间 T = 0.58 年  
- 不分红 (q = 0)  

计算过程如下：  

1. 计算 d1 和 d2：  
\[
d1 = \frac{\ln(S/K) + (r + \sigma^2/2)T}{\sigma \sqrt{T}}
\]  
\[
d2 = d1 - \sigma \sqrt{T}
\]  

先算：  
\(\ln(103.7 / 97.5) = \ln(1.0635897) \approx 0.06166\)  
\(\sigma^2/2 = 0.276^2 / 2 = 0.038088\)  
\( (r + \sigma^2/2)T = (0.043 + 0.038088) \times 0.58 = 0.081088 \times 0.58 \approx 0.047031\)  
\(\sigma \sqrt{T} = 0.276 \times \sqrt{0.58} = 0.276 \times 0.761577 \approx 0.2102\)  

所以：  
\[
d1 = \frac{0.06166 + 0.047031}{0.2102} \approx \frac{0.108691}{0.2102} \approx 0.5171
\]  
\[
d2 = 0.5171 - 0.2102 = 0.3069
\]  

2. 查标准正态分布累积函数：  
\(N(d1) \approx N(0.5171) \approx 0.6975\)  
\(N(d2) \approx N(0.3069) \approx 0.6205\)  

3. 看涨期权价格：  
\[
C = S N(d1) - K e^{-rT} N(d2)
\]  
\(e^{-rT} = e^{-0.043 \times 0.58} = e^{-0.02494} \approx 0.97537\)  
\[
C = 103.7 \times 0.6975 - 97.5 \times 0.97537 \times 0.6205
\]  
\[
= 72.33075 - (97.5 \times 0.97537 \times 0.6205)
\]  
先算 \(97.5 \times 0.97537 \approx 95.0986\)  
再乘0.6205 ≈ 59.028  
所以：  
\[
C \approx 72.3308 - 59.028 \approx 13.3028
\]  

4. Delta：  
\[
\Delta = N(d1) \approx 0.6975
\]  

5. Vega（每变动1个波动率百分点，即0.01）：  
\[
\nu = S \sqrt{T} \, N'(d1) \times 0.01
\]  
其中 \(N'(d1) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-d1^2/2}\)  
\(d1^2 = 0.5171^2 \approx 0.2674\)  
\(e^{-0.2674/2} = e^{-0.1337} \approx 0.8748\)  
\(N'(d1) = 0.39894 \times 0.8748 \approx 0.3490\)  
所以：  
\[
\nu = 103.7 \times \sqrt{0.58} \times 0.3490 \times 0.01
\]  
\(\sqrt{0.58} \approx 0.7616\)  
\(103.7 \times 0.7616 \approx 78.977\)  
再乘0.3490 ≈ 27.564  
最后乘0.01得 0.27564  

即 Vega ≈ 0.2756 （表示波动率每上升1个百分点，期权价格上升约0.2756元）
