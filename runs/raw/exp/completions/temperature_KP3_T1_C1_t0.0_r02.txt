好的，我们先一步步计算，最后把结果存入你要求的字典。  
假设这是一只每年付息一次的普通债券。

**已知条件：**  
- 面值 \( F = 100 \)  
- 票面利率 \( c = 4.6\% \) → 每年利息 \( C = 4.6 \)  
- 期限 \( n = 7 \) 年  
- 到期收益率 \( y = 5.3\% = 0.053 \)

### 1. 价格（Price）
价格公式：
\[
P = \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{C}{(1+y)^t} + \frac{F}{(1+y)^n}
\]

计算每项：

- 利息现值：
\[
\frac{4.6}{1.053} = 4.36847
\]
\[
\frac{4.6}{1.053^2} = \frac{4.6}{1.108809} \approx 4.148
\]
\[
\frac{4.6}{1.053^3} = \frac{4.6}{1.167576} \approx 3.939
\]
\[
\frac{4.6}{1.053^4} = \frac{4.6}{1.229457} \approx 3.741
\]
\[
\frac{4.6}{1.053^5} = \frac{4.6}{1.294618} \approx 3.553
\]
\[
\frac{4.6}{1.053^6} = \frac{4.6}{1.363233} \approx 3.375
\]
\[
\frac{4.6}{1.053^7} = \frac{4.6}{1.435485} \approx 3.205
\]

- 本金现值：
\[
\frac{100}{1.053^7} = \frac{100}{1.435485} \approx 69.662
\]

加总：
\[
P = 4.368 + 4.148 + 3.939 + 3.741 + 3.553 + 3.375 + 3.205 + 69.662
\]
\[
P \approx 95.991
\]

所以：
\[
\text{price} \approx 95.991
\]

### 2. Macaulay 久期（年）
公式：
\[
D_{\text{Mac}} = \frac{1}{P} \sum_{t=1}^{n} t \cdot \frac{C}{(1+y)^t} + n \cdot \frac{F}{(1+y)^n}
\]

先算分子各项：

- \( t=1: 1 \times 4.36847 = 4.36847 \)  
- \( t=2: 2 \times 4.148 = 8.296 \)  
- \( t=3: 3 \times 3.939 = 11.817 \)  
- \( t=4: 4 \times 3.741 = 14.964 \)  
- \( t=5: 5 \times 3.553 = 17.765 \)  
- \( t=6: 6 \times 3.375 = 20.250 \)  
- \( t=7: 7 \times 3.205 = 22.435 \)  
- 本金部分：\( 7 \times 69.662 = 487.634 \)

分子总和：
\[
4.368 + 8.296 + 11.817 + 14.964 + 17.765 + 20.250 + 22.435 + 487.634 = 587.529
\]

久期：
\[
D_{\text{Mac}} = \frac{587.529}{95.991} \approx 6.120 \text{ 年}
\]

---

### 3. 修正久期（Modified Duration）
公式：
\[
D_{\text{Mod}} = \frac{D_{\text{Mac}}}{1+y}
\]
\[
D_{\text{Mod}} = \frac{6.120}{1.053} \approx 5.812
\]

### 4. 凸性（Convexity）
公式：
\[
\text{Convexity} = \frac{1}{P(1+y)^2} \sum_{t=1}^{n} t(t+1) \frac{C}{(1+y)^t} + \frac{n(n+1)F}{(1+y)^n}
\]

先算各期 \( t(t+1) \times \) 现值：

- \( t=1: 1\times2 \times 4.36847 = 8.73694 \)  
- \( t=2: 2\times3 \times 4.148 = 24.888 \)  
- \( t=3: 3\times4 \times 3.939 = 47.268 \)  
- \( t=4: 4\times5 \times 3.741 = 74.820 \)

- \( t=6: 6\times7 \times 3.375 = 141.750 \)  
- \( t=7: 7\times8 \times 3.205 = 179.480 \)  
- 本金部分：\( 7\times8 \times 69.662 = 3901.072 \)

总和：
\[
8.737 + 24.888 + 47.268 + 74.820 + 106.590 + 141.750 + 179.480 + 3901.072
\]
\[
= 4484.605
\]

凸性：
\[
\text{Convexity} = \frac{4484.605}{95.991 \times (1.053)^2}
\]
\[
(1.053)^2 = 1.108809
\]
\[
95.991 \times 1.108809 \approx 106.443
\]
\[
\text{Convexity} \approx \frac{4484.605}{106.443} \approx 42.13
\]
