好的，我们一步步来推导这个最小方差组合（MVP）。  
已知三只资产的年化波动率与相关系数：

- σ₁ = 18.7% = 0.187  
- σ₂ = 24.3% = 0.243  
- σ₃ = 31.2% = 0.312  

相关系数矩阵：

\[
\rho_{12}=0.21,\quad \rho_{13}=-0.13,\quad \rho_{23}=0.37
\]

先计算协方差矩阵元素：

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{11} &= \sigma_1^2 = 0.187^2 = 0.034969 \\
\sigma_{22} &= \sigma_2^2 = 0.243^2 = 0.059049 \\
\sigma_{33} &= \sigma_3^2 = 0.312^2 = 0.097344 \\
\sigma_{12} &= \rho_{12} \sigma_1 \sigma_2 = 0.21 \times 0.187 \times 0.243 = 0.00953661 \\
\sigma_{13} &= \rho_{13} \sigma_1 \sigma_3 = -0.13 \times 0.187 \times 0.312 = -0.00758472 \\
\sigma_{23} &= \rho_{23} \sigma_2 \sigma_3 = 0.37 \times 0.243 \times 0.312 = 0.02805432
\end{aligned}
\]

所以协方差矩阵为：

\[
\Sigma = 
\begin{bmatrix}
0.034969 & 0.00953661 & -0.00758472 \\
0.00953661 & 0.059049 & 0.02805432 \\
-0.00758472 & 0.02805432 & 0.097344
\end{bmatrix}
\]

最小方差组合的权重公式（允许卖空，满仓）：

\[
\mathbf{w} = \frac{\Sigma^{-1} \mathbf{1}}{\mathbf{1}^T \Sigma^{-1} \mathbf{1}}
\]

先求逆矩阵。我们直接计算：

设  
a = 0.034969  
b = 0.00953661  
c = -0.00758472  
d = 0.059049  
e = 0.02805432  
f = 0.097344  

行列式 det(Σ) 计算：

先算：

\[
\begin{aligned}
& a(d f - e^2) = 0.034969 \times (0.059049 \times 0.097344 - 0.02805432^2) \\
& = 0.034969 \times (0.005748 - 0.000787) \\
& = 0.034969 \times 0.004961 \approx 0.0001735
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
& - b (b f - c e) = -0.00953661 \times (0.00953661\times0.097344 - (-0.00758472)\times0.02805432) \\
& = -0.00953661 \times (0.0009285 + 0.0002128) \\
& = -0.00953661 \times 0.0011413 \approx -0.00001088
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
& + c (b e - c d) = -0.00758472 \times (0.00953661\times0.02805432 - (-0.00758472)\times0.059049) \\
& = -0.00758472 \times (0.0002676 + 0.0004479) \\
& = -0.00758472 \times 0.0007155 \approx -0.000005426
\end{aligned}
\]

所以：

\[
\det(\Sigma) \approx 0.0001735 - 0.00001088 - 0.000005426 = 0.000157194
\]

现在求逆矩阵的每个元素。  
逆矩阵公式：  
\[
\Sigma^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det} \cdot \text{adj}(\Sigma)
\]

伴随矩阵元素计算：

C₁₁ = d f - e² = 0.059049×0.097344 - 0.02805432²  
= 0.005748 - 0.000787 = 0.004961  

C₁₂ = -(b f - c e) = -(0.00953661×0.097344 - (-0.00758472)×0.02805432)  
= -(0.0009285 + 0.0002128) = -0.0011413  

C₁₃ = b e - c d = 0.00953661×0.02805432 - (-0.00758472)×0.059049  
= 0.0002676 + 0.0004479 = 0.0007155  

C₂₁ = C₁₂ = -0.0011413  

C₂₂ = a f - c² = 0.034969×0.097344 - (-0.00758472)²  
= 0.003404 - 0.00005753 = 0.00334647  

C₂₃ = -(a e - b c) = -(0.034969×0.02805432 - 0.00953661×(-0.00758472))  
= -(0.0009812 + 0.00007234) = -0.00105354  

C₃₁ = C₁₃ = 0.0007155  

C₃₂ = C₂₃ = -0.00105354  

C₃₃ = a d - b² = 0.034969×0.059049 - 0.00953661²  
= 0.002065 - 0.00009095 = 0.00197405  

所以伴随矩阵为：

\[
\text{adj}(\Sigma) = 
\begin{bmatrix}
0.004961 & -0.0011413 & 0.0007155 \\
-0.0011413 & 0.00334647 & -0.00105354 \\
0.0007155 & -0.00105354 & 0.00197405
\end{bmatrix}
\]

逆矩阵为：

\[
\Sigma^{-1} = \frac{1}{0.000157194} \times \text{adj}(\Sigma)
\]

分别计算：

第一行：  
inv11 = 0.004961 / 0.000157194 ≈ 31.561  
inv12 = -0.0011413 / 0.000157194 ≈ -7.259  
inv13 = 0.0007155 / 0.000157194 ≈ 4.551  

第二行：  
inv21 = -7.259  
inv22 = 0.00334647 / 0.000157194 ≈ 21.288  
inv23 = -0.00105354 / 0.000157194 ≈ -6.702  

第三行：  
inv31 = 4.551  
inv32 = -6.702  
inv33 = 0.00197405 / 0.000157194 ≈ 12.557  

所以：

\[
\Sigma^{-1} \approx 
\begin{bmatrix}
31.561 & -7.259 & 4.551 \\
-7.259 & 21.288 & -6.702 \\
4.551 & -6.702 & 12.557
\end{bmatrix}
\]

现在求全1向量与逆矩阵乘积：

\[
\Sigma^{-1} \mathbf{1} = 
\begin{bmatrix}
31.561 - 7.259 + 4.551 = 28.853 \\
-7.259 + 21.288 - 6.702 = 7.327 \\
4.551 - 6.702 + 12.557 = 10.406
\end{bmatrix}
\]

分母 = 28.853 + 7.327 + 10.406 = 46.586  

所以权重：

\[
w_1 = 28.853 / 46.586 \approx 0.6193 \\
w_2 = 7.327 / 46.586 \approx 0.1573 \\
w_3 = 10.406 / 46.586 \approx 0.2234
\]

验证总和 ≈ 1。

现在计算组合方差：

\[
\sigma_p^2 = \mathbf{w}^T \Sigma \mathbf{w}
\]

先计算 Σw：

第一分量：  
0.034969×0.6193 + 0.00953661×0.1573 + (-0.00758472)×0.2234  
= 0.021659 + 0.001500 - 0.001694 ≈ 0.021465  

第二分量：  
0.00953661×0.6193 + 0.059049×0.1573 + 0.02805432×0.2234  
= 0.005906 + 0.009287 + 0.006267 ≈ 0.021460  

第三分量：  
-0.00758472×0.6193 + 0.02805432×0.1573 + 0.097344×0.2234  
= -0.004697 + 0.004413 + 0.021748 ≈ 0.021464  

然后 w·(Σw) =  
0.6193×0.021465 + 0.1573×0.021460 + 0.2234×0.021464  
= 0.013293 + 0.003376 + 0.004795 = 0.021464  

所以年化波动率 = sqrt(0.021464) ≈ 0.1465 = 14.65%

result = {
