要计算投资组合的波动率，我们使用两资产组合的方差公式：
$$ \sigma_p^2 = w_A^2 \sigma_A^2 + w_B^2 \sigma_B^2 + 2 w_A w_B \sigma_A \sigma_B \rho $$

**已知参数：**
* 资产A波动率 $\sigma_A = 18.4\% = 0.184$
* 资产B波动率 $\sigma_B = 29.7\% = 0.297$
* 权重 $w_A = 0.6$，$w_B = 0.4$（六四开）

**计算固定项：**
* $w_A^2 \sigma_A^2 = 0.6^2 \times 0.184^2 = 0.36 \times 0.033856 = 0.01218816$
* $w_B^2 \sigma_B^2 = 0.4^2 \times 0.297^2 = 0.16 \times 0.088209 = 0.01411344$
* $2 w_A w_B \sigma_A \sigma_B = 2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 \times 0.184 \times 0.297 = 0.48 \times 0.054648 = 0.02623104$

**1. 相关性飙升前（$\rho = 0.3$）：**
$$ \sigma_{p, before}^2 = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.3 \times 0.02623104 = 0.0263016 + 0.007869312 = 0.034170912 $$
$$ \sigma_{p, before} = \sqrt{0.034170912} \approx 0.18485 \text{ （即 18.485\%)} $$

**2. 相关性飙升后（$\rho = 0.8$）：**
$$ \sigma_{p, after}^2 = 0.0263016 + 0.8 \times 0.02623104 = 0.0263016 + 0.020984832 = 0.047286432 $$
$$ \sigma_{p, after} = \sqrt{0.047286432} \approx 0.21745 \text{ （即 21.745\%)} $$
