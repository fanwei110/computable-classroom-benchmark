根据投资组合波动率公式，组合年化波动率 \(\sigma_p = \sqrt{w_A^2 \sigma_A^2 + w_B^2 \sigma_B^2 + 2 w_A w_B \rho \sigma_A \sigma_B}\)。  
已知 \(w_A = 0.6\)，\(w_B = 0.4\)，\(\sigma_A = 0.184\)，\(\sigma_B = 0.297\)，相关系数变化前后分别为 \(\rho_{\text{before}} = 0.3\) 和 \(\rho_{\text{after}} = 0.8\)。

**计算过程：**
- \(w_A^2\sigma_A^2 = 0.6^2 \times 0.184^2 = 0.36 \times 0.033856 = 0.01218816\)
- \(w_B^2\sigma_B^2 = 0.4^2 \times 0.297^2 = 0.16 \times 0.088209 = 0.01411344\)
- 之前协方差项：\(2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 \times 0.3 \times 0.184 \times 0.297 = 0.007869312\)
- 之后协方差项：\(2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 \times 0.8 \times 0.184 \times 0.297 = 0.020984832\)

之前组合方差：\(0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.007869312 = 0.034170912\)  
之前组合波动率：\(\sqrt{0.034170912} \approx 0.184854\) (即 18.49%)

之后组合方差：\(0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.020984832 = 0.047286432\)
