根据投资组合波动率的计算公式：
$$ \sigma_p = \sqrt{w_A^2 \sigma_A^2 + w_B^2 \sigma_B^2 + 2 w_A w_B \rho_{A,B} \sigma_A \sigma_B} $$

已知参数：
- $w_A = 0.6$, $w_B = 0.4$ （六四开组合）
- $\sigma_A = 18.4\% = 0.184$
- $\sigma_B = 29.7\% = 0.297$
- 相关性变化前 $\rho_{before} = 0.3$，变化后 $\rho_{after} = 0.8$

**计算过程：**
1. $w_A^2 \sigma_A^2 = 0.6^2 \times 0.184^2 = 0.36 \times 0.033856 = 0.01218816$
2. $w_B^2 \sigma_B^2 = 0.4^2 \times 0.297^2 = 0.16 \times 0.088209 = 0.01411344$
3. $2 w_A w_B \sigma_A \sigma_B = 2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 \times 0.184 \times 0.297 = 0.48 \times 0.054648 = 0.02623104$

**相关性为 0.3 时的组合方差与波动率：**
- $Var_{before} = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.02623104 \times 0.3 = 0.0263016 + 0.007869312 = 0.034170912$
- $\sigma_{before} = \sqrt{0.034170912} \approx 0.1848537$，即 **18.49%**

**相关性为 0.8 时的组合方差与波动率：**
- $Var_{after} = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.02623104 \times 0.8 = 0.0263016 + 0.020984832 = 0.047286432$
- $\sigma_{after} = \sqrt{0.047286432} \approx 0.2174542$，即 **21.75%**
