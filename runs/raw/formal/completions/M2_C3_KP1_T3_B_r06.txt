组合波动率（年化）计算如下：

- 权重：A 占 60%，B 占 40%（“六四开”）
- 波动率：σ_A = 0.184，σ_B = 0.297
- 相关系数：ρ₁ = 0.3（前），ρ₂ = 0.8（后）

组合方差公式：
\[
\sigma_p^2 = w_A^2\sigma_A^2 + w_B^2\sigma_B^2 + 2w_A w_B\rho\sigma_A\sigma_B
\]

**计算过程：**
\[
w_A^2\sigma_A^2 = 0.6^2 \times 0.184^2 = 0.36 \times 0.033856 = 0.01218816
\]
\[
w_B^2\sigma_B^2 = 0.4^2 \times 0.297^2 = 0.16 \times 0.088209 = 0.01411344
\]
\[
2w_A w_B \sigma_A \sigma_B = 2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 \times 0.184 \times 0.297 = 0.48 \times 0.054648 = 0.02623104
\]

- 相关系数 0.3 时：
  \[
  \sigma_{p1}^2 = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.3 \times 0.02623104 = 0.034170912
  \]
  \[
  \sigma_{p1} = \sqrt{0.034170912} \approx 0.184854
  \]

- 相关系数 0.8 时：
  \[
  \sigma_{p2}^2 = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.8 \times 0.02623104 = 0.047286432
  \]
  \[
  \sigma_{p2} = \sqrt{0.047286432} \approx 0.217455
  \]
