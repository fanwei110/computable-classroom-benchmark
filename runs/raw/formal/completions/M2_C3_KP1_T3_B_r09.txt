根据题目信息，资产 A 波动率 \(\sigma_A = 18.4\%\)，资产 B 波动率 \(\sigma_B = 29.7\%\)，组合权重采用“六四开”（按提及顺序，A 占 60%，B 占 40%），相关性从 \(\rho_{\text{before}} = 0.3\) 升至 \(\rho_{\text{after}} = 0.8\)。组合年化波动率公式为：
\[
\sigma_p = \sqrt{w_A^2\sigma_A^2 + w_B^2\sigma_B^2 + 2w_A w_B \rho \sigma_A\sigma_B}
\]

计算过程：
- \(w_A = 0.6\), \(w_B = 0.4\)
- \(\sigma_A = 0.184\), \(\sigma_A^2 = 0.033856\)
- \(\sigma_B = 0.297\), \(\sigma_B^2 = 0.088209\)
- \(w_A^2\sigma_A^2 = 0.6^2 \times 0.033856 = 0.01218816\)
- \(w_B^2\sigma_B^2 = 0.4^2 \times 0.088209 = 0.01411344\)
- \(2 w_A w_B \sigma_A \sigma_B = 2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 \times 0.184 \times 0.297 = 0.054648 \times 0.48 = 0.02623104\)（系数部分）

**ρ=0.3 时**：
方差 = \(0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.3 \times 0.02623104 = 0.034170912\)
波动率 = \(\sqrt{0.034170912} \approx 0.18486\)

**ρ=0.8 时**：
方差 = \(0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.8 \times 0.02623104 = 0.047286432\)
波动率 = \(\sqrt{0.047286432} \approx 0.21746\)
