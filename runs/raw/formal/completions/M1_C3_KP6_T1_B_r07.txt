### 2. 归因分析（Allocation, Selection, Interaction Effects）
给定：
- 组合权重：`w_p = [0.45, 0.35, 0.2]`
- 行业收益：`r_p = [0.083, 0.021, -0.014]`
- 基准权重：`w_b = [0.4, 0.4, 0.2]`
- 基准收益：`r_b = [0.067, 0.034, -0.009]`

归因分析的三个效应计算如下：

#### (1) 配置效应（Allocation Effect）
\[
\text{Allocation Effect} = \sum (w_{p,i} - w_{b,i}) \times r_{b,i}
\]
计算：
\[
(0.45 - 0.4) \times 0.067 + (0.35 - 0.4) \times 0.034 + (0.2 - 0.2) \times (-0.009) = 0.00335 - 0.0017 + 0 = 0.00165
\]

#### (2) 选择效应（Selection Effect）
\[
\text{Selection Effect} = \sum w_{b,i} \times (r_{p,i} - r_{b,i})
\]
计算：
\[
0.4 \times (0.083 - 0.067) + 0.4 \times (0.021 - 0.034) + 0.2 \times (-0.014 - (-0.009)) = 0.0064 - 0.0052 - 0.001 = 0.0002
\]

#### (3) 交互效应（Interaction Effect）
\[
\text{Interaction Effect} = \sum (w_{p,i} - w_{b,i}) \times (r_{p,i} - r_{b,i})
\]
计算：
\[
(0.45 - 0.4) \times (0.083 - 0.067) + (0.35 - 0.4) \times (0.021 - 0.034) + (0.2 - 0.2) \times (-0.014 - (-0.009)) = 0.0008 + 0.00065 + 0 = 0.00145
\]
