第一问缺少 `fund` 列的日收益数据，无法计算夏普比率，因此 `sharpe_annual` 设为 `None`。第二问的业绩归因计算如下：

**基准总收益**  
\( R_b = 0.40 \times 6.7\% + 0.40 \times 3.4\% + 0.20 \times (-0.9\%) = 3.86\% \)  

**组合总收益**  
\( R_p = 0.45 \times 8.3\% + 0.35 \times 2.1\% + 0.20 \times (-1.4\%) = 4.19\% \)  

**配置效应 (Allocation Effect)**  
\[
\begin{aligned}
&= \sum (w_{p,i} - w_{b,i}) \times (R_{b,i} - R_b) \\
&= (0.45-0.40)(6.7\%-3.86\%) + (0.35-0.40)(3.4\%-3.86\%) + (0.20-0.20)(-0.9\%-3.86\%) \\
&= 0.05 \times 2.84\% + (-0.05) \times (-0.46\%) + 0 \\
&= 0.142\% + 0.023\% = 0.165\%
\end{aligned}
\]

**选择效应 (Selection Effect)**  
\[
\begin{aligned}
&= \sum w_{b,i} \times (R_{p,i} - R_{b,i}) \\
&= 0.40 \times (8.3\%-6.7\%) + 0.40 \times (2.1\%-3.4\%) + 0.20 \times (-1.4\%+0.9\%) \\
&= 0.40 \times 1.6\% + 0.40 \times (-1.3\%) + 0.20 \times (-0.5\%) \\
&= 0.64\% - 0.52\% - 0.10\% = 0.02\%
\end{aligned}
\]

**交互效应 (Interaction Effect)**  
\[
\begin{aligned}
&= \sum (w_{p,i} - w_{b,i}) \times (R_{p,i} - R_{b,i}) \\
&= 0.05 \times 1.6\% + (-0.05) \times (-1.3\%) + 0 \times (-0.5\%) \\
&= 0.08\% + 0.065\% = 0.145\%
\end{aligned}
\]
