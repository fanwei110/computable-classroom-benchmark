根据投资组合波动率的计算公式：
$$ \sigma_p = \sqrt{w_A^2 \sigma_A^2 + w_B^2 \sigma_B^2 + 2 w_A w_B \sigma_A \sigma_B \rho} $$

已知条件：
- 资产A波动率 $\sigma_A = 18.4\% = 0.184$
- 资产B波动率 $\sigma_B = 29.7\% = 0.297$
- 权重 $w_A = 0.6$, $w_B = 0.4$

先计算公式中的常数项：
- $w_A^2 \sigma_A^2 = 0.6^2 \times 0.184^2 = 0.01218816$
- $w_B^2 \sigma_B^2 = 0.4^2 \times 0.297^2 = 0.01411344$
- $2 w_A w_B \sigma_A \sigma_B = 2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 \times 0.184 \times 0.297 = 0.02623104$

**1. 相关性改变前（$\rho = 0.3$）：**
$$ \sigma_{p, before}^2 = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.02623104 \times 0.3 = 0.034170912 $$
$$ \sigma_{p, before} = \sqrt{0.034170912} \approx 0.184908 \approx 18.49\% $$

**2. 相关性改变后（$\rho = 0.8$）：**
$$ \sigma_{p, after}^2 = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.02623104 \times 0.8 = 0.047286432 $$
$$ \sigma_{p, after} = \sqrt{0.047286432} \approx 0.217454 \approx 21.75\% $$
