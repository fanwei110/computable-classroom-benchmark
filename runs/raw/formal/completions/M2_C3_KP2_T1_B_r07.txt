### 1. CAPM 公式
\[
ER_i = r_f + \beta_i (ER_m - r_f)
\]
已知：
- \( r_f = 0.023 \)
- \( ER_m = 0.094 \)
- 市场风险溢价： \( 0.094 - 0.023 = 0.071 \)

---

**股票 X：**  
\(\beta_x = 0.62\)  
\[
ER_x = 0.023 + 0.62 \times 0.071 = 0.023 + 0.04402 = 0.06702
\]

---

**股票 Y：**  
\(\beta_y = 1.18\)  
\[
ER_y = 0.023 + 1.18 \times 0.071 = 0.023 + 0.08378 = 0.10678
\]

---

**股票 Z：**  
\(\beta_z = 1.51\)  
\[
ER_z = 0.023 + 1.51 \times 0.071 = 0.023 + 0.10721 = 0.13021
\]

### 2. 计算 Y 的 Alpha  
实际收益： \(0.131\)
