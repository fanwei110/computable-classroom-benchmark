根据 Black-Scholes 欧式看涨期权定价公式，以及给定的计算约定，我们推导如下：

**参数设定：**
- 标的现价 \( S = 103.7 \)
- 行权价 \( K = 97.5 \)
- 隐含波动率 \( \sigma = 27.6\% = 0.276 \)
- 无风险利率（年复利报价）\( r_{\text{bond}} = 4.3\% = 0.043 \)
- 剩余期限 \( T = 0.58 \) 年
- 无红利 \( q = 0 \)

按约定将年复利转化为连续复利：
\[ r = \ln(1 + r_{\text{bond}}) = \ln(1.043) \approx 0.0421115 \]

**计算 \( d_1 \) 和 \( d_2 \)：**
\[ d_1 = \frac{\ln(S/K) + \left( r + \frac{\sigma^2}{2} \right)T}{\sigma\sqrt{T}} \]
\[ \ln(103.7 / 97.5) \approx 0.0616546 \]
\[ r + \frac{\sigma^2}{2} = 0.0421115 + \frac{0.276^2}{2} = 0.0421115 + 0.038088 = 0.0801995 \]
\[ \left( r + \frac{\sigma^2}{2} \right)T = 0.0801995 \times 0.58 \approx 0.0465157 \]
分子 = \( 0.0616546 + 0.0465157 = 0.1081703 \)
\[ \sigma\sqrt{T} = 0.276 \times \sqrt{0.58} \approx 0.276 \times 0.7615773 \approx 0.2101953 \]
\[ d_1 \approx 0.1081703 / 0.2101953 \approx 0.51463 \]
\[ d_2 = d_1 - \sigma\sqrt{T} \approx 0.51463 - 0.21020 = 0.30443 \]

**计算价格、Delta、Vega：**
标准正态累积分布和密度函数：
\[ N(d_1) \approx 0.69659, \quad N(d_2) \approx 0.61960 \]
\[ N'(d_1) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-d_1^2/2} \approx 0.34948 \]

**看涨期权价格：**
\[ C = S N(d_1) - K e^{-rT} N(d_2) \]
\[ e^{-rT} = e^{-0.0421115 \times 0.58} \approx 0.97587 \]
\[ C \approx 103.7 \times 0.69659 - 97.5 \times 0.97587 \times 0.61960 \approx 72.2364 - 58.9535 \approx 13.2829 \]

**Delta：**
\[ \Delta_{\text{call}} = N(d_1) \approx 0.69659 \]

**Vega（波动率绝对变动的价格敏感度）：**
\[ \mathcal{V} = S \sqrt{T} N'(d_1) \approx 103.7 \times 0.7615773 \times 0.34948 \approx 27.5996 \]

（注：若题目期望的是每 1% 波动率变化的 Vega，则需乘以 0.01 得到 0.2760；此处采用标准 Black-Scholes 偏导数的学术定义。）
