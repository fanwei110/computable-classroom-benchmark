根据投资组合方差公式：
$$ \sigma_p^2 = w_A^2 \sigma_A^2 + w_B^2 \sigma_B^2 + 2 w_A w_B \sigma_A \sigma_B \rho $$

**已知条件：**
* 资产权重：$w_A = 0.6$, $w_B = 0.4$ （六四开组合）
* 资产波动率：$\sigma_A = 18.4\% = 0.184$, $\sigma_B = 29.7\% = 0.297$
* 相关性变化前：$\rho_{before} = 0.3$
* 相关性变化后：$\rho_{after} = 0.8$

**1. 计算变化前组合波动率（相关性为0.3）：**
$$ \sigma_{p, before}^2 = (0.6^2 \times 0.184^2) + (0.4^2 \times 0.297^2) + 2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 \times 0.184 \times 0.297 \times 0.3 $$
$$ \sigma_{p, before}^2 = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.007869312 = 0.034170912 $$
$$ \sigma_{p, before} = \sqrt{0.034170912} \approx 0.18488 \text{ (即 18.49\%)} $$

**2. 计算变化后组合波动率（相关性为0.8）：**
$$ \sigma_{p, after}^2 = (0.6^2 \times 0.184^2) + (0.4^2 \times 0.297^2) + 2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 \times 0.184 \times 0.297 \times 0.8 $$
$$ \sigma_{p, after}^2 = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.020984832 = 0.047286432 $$
$$ \sigma_{p, after} = \sqrt{0.047286432} \approx 0.21746 \text{ (即 21.75\%)} $$
