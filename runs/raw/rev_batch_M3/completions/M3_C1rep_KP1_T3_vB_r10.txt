根据投资组合波动率的计算公式：
$\sigma_p = \sqrt{w_A^2 \sigma_A^2 + w_B^2 \sigma_B^2 + 2 w_A w_B \sigma_A \sigma_B \rho}$

已知条件：
- $w_A = 0.6$, $w_B = 0.4$
- $\sigma_A = 18.4\% = 0.184$
- $\sigma_B = 29.7\% = 0.297$

**1. 计算波动前（相关性 $\rho = 0.3$）：**
$\sigma_{before}^2 = 0.6^2 \times 0.184^2 + 0.4^2 \times 0.297^2 + 2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 \times 0.184 \times 0.297 \times 0.3$
$\sigma_{before}^2 = 0.36 \times 0.033856 + 0.16 \times 0.088209 + 0.48 \times 0.054648 \times 0.3$
$\sigma_{before}^2 = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.007869312 = 0.034170912$
$\sigma_{before} = \sqrt{0.034170912} \approx 0.184854$ 即 **18.4854%**

**2. 计算波动后（相关性 $\rho = 0.8$）：**
$\sigma_{after}^2 = 0.6^2 \times 0.184^2 + 0.4^2 \times 0.297^2 + 2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 \times 0.184 \times 0.297 \times 0.8$
$\sigma_{after}^2 = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.020984832 = 0.047286432$
$\sigma_{after} = \sqrt{0.047286432} \approx 0.217457$ 即 **21.7457%**
