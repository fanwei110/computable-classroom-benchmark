根据投资组合波动率的计算公式：
$$ \sigma_p = \sqrt{w_A^2 \sigma_A^2 + w_B^2 \sigma_B^2 + 2 w_A w_B \rho \sigma_A \sigma_B} $$

已知参数：
- 资产A波动率 $\sigma_A = 18.4\% = 0.184$
- 资产B波动率 $\sigma_B = 29.7\% = 0.297$
- 权重 $w_A = 0.6$，$w_B = 0.4$

**1. 计算相关性变化前的组合波动率（$\rho = 0.3$）：**
$$ \sigma_{p, before}^2 = (0.6)^2(0.184)^2 + (0.4)^2(0.297)^2 + 2(0.6)(0.4)(0.3)(0.184)(0.297) $$
$$ \sigma_{p, before}^2 = 0.36 \times 0.033856 + 0.16 \times 0.088209 + 0.48 \times 0.3 \times 0.054648 $$
$$ \sigma_{p, before}^2 = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.007869312 = 0.034170912 $$
$$ \sigma_{p, before} = \sqrt{0.034170912} \approx 0.184421 \approx 18.44\% $$

**2. 计算相关性变化后的组合波动率（$\rho = 0.8$）：**
$$ \sigma_{p, after}^2 = (0.6)^2(0.184)^2 + (0.4)^2(0.297)^2 + 2(0.6)(0.4)(0.8)(0.184)(0.297) $$
$$ \sigma_{p, after}^2 = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.48 \times 0.8 \times 0.054648 $$
$$ \sigma_{p, after}^2 = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.020984832 = 0.047286432 $$
$$ \sigma_{p, after} = \sqrt{0.047286432} \approx 0.217454 \approx 21.75\% $$
