根据Black-Scholes期权定价模型，针对不分红的欧式看涨期权，计算过程如下：

**已知参数：**
*   标的现价 ($S$) = 103.7
*   行权价 ($K$) = 97.5
*   隐含波动率 ($\sigma$) = 0.276
*   无风险利率 ($r$) = 0.043
*   剩余时间 ($T$) = 0.58 年

**计算中间变量：**
*   $d_1 = \frac{\ln(S/K) + (r + \sigma^2/2)T}{\sigma\sqrt{T}} = \frac{\ln(103.7/97.5) + (0.043 + 0.276^2/2) \times 0.58}{0.276 \times \sqrt{0.58}} \approx 0.5154$
*   $d_2 = d_1 - \sigma\sqrt{T} \approx 0.5154 - 0.2106 \approx 0.3048$

**计算期权希腊值与价格：**
*   **Call Delta ($\Delta$)** = $N(d_1) = N(0.5154) \approx 0.6967$
*   **Call Vega** = $S \sqrt{T} N'(d_1)$，其中 $N'(d_1) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-d_1^2/2} \approx 0.3493$
    Call Vega = $103.7 \times \sqrt{0.58} \times 0.3493 \approx 27.6516$ *(注：此为波动率每变动1个大点(100%)的价格变动，若按惯例每1%变动则除以100，即0.2765)*
*   **Call Price ($C$)** = $S \cdot N(d_1) - K \cdot e^{-rT} \cdot N(d_2)$
    = $103.7 \times 0.6967 - 97.5 \times e^{-0.043 \times 0.58} \times 0.6198 \approx 72.3396 - 58.9466 \approx 13.3930$
