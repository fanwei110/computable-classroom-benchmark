根据投资组合波动率的计算公式：
$$ \sigma_P = \sqrt{w_A^2 \sigma_A^2 + w_B^2 \sigma_B^2 + 2 w_A w_B \rho \sigma_A \sigma_B} $$

已知条件：
- A的权重 $w_A = 0.6$，波动率 $\sigma_A = 18.4\% = 0.184$
- B的权重 $w_B = 0.4$，波动率 $\sigma_B = 29.7\% = 0.297$

首先计算公式中的共有部分：
- $w_A^2 \sigma_A^2 = 0.6^2 \times 0.184^2 = 0.36 \times 0.033856 = 0.01218816$
- $w_B^2 \sigma_B^2 = 0.4^2 \times 0.297^2 = 0.16 \times 0.088209 = 0.01411344$
- $2 w_A w_B \sigma_A \sigma_B = 2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 \times 0.184 \times 0.297 = 0.48 \times 0.054648 = 0.02623104$

**1. 相关系数 $\rho = 0.3$ 时的组合波动率（前）：**
$$ \sigma_{before}^2 = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.3 \times 0.02623104 = 0.0263016 + 0.007869312 = 0.034170912 $$
$$ \sigma_{before} = \sqrt{0.034170912} \approx 0.1848537 \approx 18.49\% $$

**2. 相关系数 $\rho = 0.8$ 时的组合波动率（后）：**
$$ \sigma_{after}^2 = 0.01218816 + 0.01411344 + 0.8 \times 0.02623104 = 0.0263016 + 0.020984832 = 0.047286432 $$
$$ \sigma_{after} = \sqrt{0.047286432} \approx 0.2174542 \approx 21.75\% $$
