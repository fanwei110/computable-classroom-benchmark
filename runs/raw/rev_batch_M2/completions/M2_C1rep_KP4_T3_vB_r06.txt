计算 BSM 模型下的 Vega 值：  
\[
d_1 = \frac{\ln(S/K) + (r + 0.5\sigma^2)T}{\sigma\sqrt{T}} \approx 0.5171

\[
N'(d_1) \approx 0.3490
\]
\[
\text{Vega} = S \cdot N'(d_1) \cdot \sqrt{T} \approx 27.564
\]

\[
