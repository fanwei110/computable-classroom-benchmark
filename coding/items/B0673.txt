根据 Black-Scholes 模型，Vega 的计算公式为：
$$Vega = S \cdot N'(d_1) \cdot \sqrt{T}$$

$$d_1 = \frac{0.061684 + 0.047031}{0.210195} \approx 0.517161$$

$$Vega = 103.7 \times 0.349004 \times 0.761577 \approx 27.5597$$

$$\text{price\_change} = Vega \times 0.01 = 27.5597 \times 0.01 \approx 0.2756$$
