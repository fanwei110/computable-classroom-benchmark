**第一部分：计算最小方差组合（ρ=0.45 时）**

公式：

\[
w_1 = \frac{\sigma_2^2 - \rho\sigma_1\sigma_2}{\sigma_1^2+\sigma_2^2-2\rho\sigma_1\sigma_2}
\]  
\[
\sigma_p = \sqrt{w_1^2\sigma_1^2 + (1-w_1)^2\sigma_2^2 + 2w_1(1-w_1)\rho\sigma_1\sigma_2}
\]

- 资产1：μ₁ = 0.071, σ₁ = 0.163  
- 资产2：μ₂ = 0.124, σ₂ = 0.289  
- ρ = 0.45  

\[
w_1 = \frac{0.289^2 - 0.45 \times 0.163 \times 0.289}{0.163^2 + 0.289^2 - 2 \times 0.45 \times 0.163 \times 0.289}
= \frac{0.083521 - 0.02121135}{0.026569 + 0.083521 - 0.0424227}
= \frac{0.06230965}{0.0676673} \approx 0.9207
\]

\[
\sigma_p = [0.9207^2 \cdot 0.163^2 + (0.0793)^2 \cdot 0.289^2 + 2 \cdot 0.9207 \cdot 0.0793 \cdot 0.45 \cdot 0.163 \cdot 0.289]^{1/2}
