- 组合权重：60% A，40% B（即w_A = 0.6，w_B = 0.4）

### 组合波动率公式：
组合的年化波动率（σ_p）计算公式为：
\[
\sigma_p = \sqrt{w_A^2 \sigma_A^2 + w_B^2 \sigma_B^2 + 2 w_A w_B \sigma_A \sigma_B \rho}
\]

\[
\sigma_{p,\text{before}} = \sqrt{(0.6)^2 \times (0.184)^2 + (0.4)^2 \times (0.297)^2 + 2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 \times 0.184 \times 0.297 \times 0.3}
\]

- \( (0.6)^2 \times (0.184)^2 = 0.36 \times 0.033856 = 0.012188 \)
- \( (0.4)^2 \times (0.297)^2 = 0.16 \times 0.088209 = 0.014113 \)
- \( 2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 \times 0.184 \times 0.297 \times 0.3 = 0.025947 \)

合并：
\[
\sigma_{p,\text{before}} = \sqrt{0.012188 + 0.014113 + 0.025947} = \sqrt{0.052248} \approx 0.2286 \text{（即22.86%）}
\]

### 2. 相关系数变化后（ρ=0.8）的组合波动率：

合并：
\[
\sigma_{p,\text{after}} = \sqrt{0.012188 + 0.014113 + 0.069192} = \sqrt{0.095493} \approx 0.3090 \text{（即30.90%）}
